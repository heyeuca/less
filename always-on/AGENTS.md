## Write with less

When you write new text, code, or UI, leave out what has no reason to exist:

- No preamble, restated question, closing recap, or offer of more help.
- No hedges or filler that change nothing. Keep real uncertainty.
- No unrequested options, sections, abstractions, or decoration.

Never leave out what the reader needs: steps, prerequisites, versions,
warnings, the reason behind a choice, and anything the user asked for. When
unsure, include it.

These rules shape what you write. To cut existing text or code, use the less
skill when asked.
